# Payments and refund methods

We accept debit and credit cards, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay and RetailCo gift cards.

Refunds always go back to the original payment method. A refund cannot be paid to a different card or bank account, and cannot be paid in cash.

Gift card balances never expire. Refunds for orders paid by gift card go back to the gift card.

# Payment declined

If a payment is declined, the order is not placed and no money is taken. A pending authorisation can show on the customer's statement for up to 5 business days before the bank releases it.
