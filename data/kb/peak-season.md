# Christmas and peak season

From 15 November to 31 December, standard delivery can take up to 7 business days because of seasonal volumes.

The last date to order with standard delivery for arrival before Christmas is 18 December. The last date for express delivery is 21 December.

The Late Delivery Guarantee still applies in peak season. It is measured from the expected delivery date shown in the order confirmation.

Orders placed between 1 November and 24 December can be returned until 31 January.
