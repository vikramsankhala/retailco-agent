# Talking to a person and making a complaint

Customers can reach a member of the support team at any time by typing "person" in chat. The team replies to chat and email within 2 hours, 8am to 10pm, seven days a week.

To make a formal complaint, the customer can ask the assistant to pass the conversation to a team leader. We acknowledge complaints within 1 business day and aim to resolve them within 14 days.
