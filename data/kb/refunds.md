# Late Delivery Guarantee and refunds

If an order arrives 7 or more days after its expected delivery date, the customer is eligible for a full refund of the order total. The refund goes back to the original payment method.

If an order is 3 to 6 days late, the customer is eligible for a £5 goodwill credit, not a refund.

Refunds above £200 need approval from a senior support agent before they are issued. The assistant must not promise a refund above £200 until it has been approved.

Refunds usually take 3 to 5 business days to reach the customer's account.
