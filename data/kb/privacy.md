# Your account and personal data

Customers can update their name, email address and saved addresses in the My Account page.

To get a copy of the personal data we hold, or to ask us to delete an account, the customer emails privacy@retailco.example. We reply within one month. The assistant cannot delete accounts or send personal data in chat.

We never ask for a full card number, a password or a security code in chat or by email. If someone asks for these, it is not RetailCo.
