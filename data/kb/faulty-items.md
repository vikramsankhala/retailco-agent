# Damaged or faulty items

If an item arrives damaged, broken or faulty, the customer can choose a replacement or a full refund for that item. Ask for a photo of the damage and the order number.

Report damage within 30 days of delivery. After 30 days, faulty items are covered by the manufacturer's warranty and handled by the returns team.

The customer does not pay to send back a damaged or faulty item. We email a prepaid label once the photo has been checked.

# Missing items

If an order arrives with an item missing, check whether the dispatch email shows a second parcel. Items from different warehouses can arrive separately, up to 2 days apart.
